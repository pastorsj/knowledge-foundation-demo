## Hardship statement

not been able to keep up with the full payment since then and I am now behind on the loan. I am asking the Bank to lower
my payment for six months while my hours are restored. My work schedule letter is attached.

I certify that the information in this request is true and complete. I understand that the Bank may verify it, that
approval is not guaranteed, that any payment I do not make during the plan is added back to my loan balance, and that
my loan stays in effect under its original terms except as the Bank agrees in writing.
