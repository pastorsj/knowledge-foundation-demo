## Overview

This disclosure describes the fees that apply to Harborview Community Bank consumer deposit and credit card accounts,
how overdrafts are handled and how to avoid fees. Fees are subject to change; the Bank will give at least 30 days'
written notice before a fee increase takes effect. Ask any branch for a printed copy, or sign in to online banking
to see the fees that apply to your accounts.

## Overdrafts

An overdraft happens when you do not have enough money in your checking account to cover a transaction and the Bank
pays it anyway. The Bank decides whether to pay an overdraft; it is not obliged to. If the Bank pays an item that
overdraws your account, the Overdraft Paid-Item Fee in Table 2 applies.

- **No fee for small overdrafts.** If your account is overdrawn by $5 or less at the end of the business day, no
  overdraft fee is charged.
- **Daily limit.** The Bank charges no more than 3 overdraft or returned-item fees in one business day.
- **ATM and everyday debit card transactions.** The Bank pays an overdraft on an ATM withdrawal or a one-time debit
  card purchase only if you have opted in. If you have not, the transaction is declined and no fee is charged.
- **Overdraft protection transfer.** You can link a Harborview savings account to your checking account. When a checking
  transaction would overdraw the account, the Bank moves the needed amount from savings and charges the transfer fee
  in Table 2 instead of the Overdraft Paid-Item Fee.
- **Returned items.** If the Bank declines to pay a check or electronic payment because of insufficient funds, the
  Returned Item Fee applies. The Bank does not charge a second fee if the same item is presented again.

**Table 2. Overdraft and returned item fees**

| Fee | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Overdraft Paid-Item Fee | $30 per item | No fee when overdrawn by $5 or less at end of day; at most 3 fees per business day |
| Returned Item Fee (insufficient funds) | $30 per item | At most 3 fees per business day, combined with Overdraft Paid-Item Fees |
| Overdraft protection transfer from savings | $10 per day | Charged once per business day that a transfer is made |

Overdraft fees are reported on your monthly statement under the heading "Fees". Customers who are overdrawn on
more than 3 occasions in a statement period receive a call from a Harborview banker to talk about options such as
Student Checking, a linked savings account or alerts for low balances.

## How to dispute a charge

If you see a card or debit transaction you did not authorize, or a charge that is wrong, call the number on your card
or the Bank's Card Services line within 60 days of the statement that shows the charge. For debit card and other
electronic transactions the Bank investigates within 10 business days and may credit your account provisionally while
it finishes, which takes no more than 45 days. See the Complaint Handling Procedure for how the Bank tracks and
resolves disputes.
