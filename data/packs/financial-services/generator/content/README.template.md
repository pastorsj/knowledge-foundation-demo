# Financial Services: Harborview Community Bank

Retail banking for NVIDIA Knowledge Foundation: a fictional community bank's customers, accounts and loans in DuckDB,
beside its credit, compliance and disclosure documents, with questions that use retrieval over the documents, SQL over
the tables and Kumo predictions.

> **This data is synthetic.** Harborview Community Bank, its customers, branches, loans and every person, figure and
> document in this pack are fictional and were generated by `generator/build.py` for a software demonstration. They
> describe no real institution or person. Nothing here is financial advice, a lending recommendation or a
> description of any real bank's policies. The regulatory references in the compliance documents are
> simplified for illustration and are not legal guidance.

### Tables (source `banking`, "Customers, Accounts & Loans")

{{rows_table}}

| Table | One row per | Key | Time column |
|---|---|---|---|
| `branches` | branch | `branch_id` | |
| `customers` | customer (segment, tenure, credit score and band, region, home branch) | `customer_id` | `customer_since` |
| `accounts` | checking, savings or credit card account (open and close dates) | `account_id` | `opened_date` |
| `monthly_balances` | account and month: ending and average balance, inflows, outflows, transactions, overdraft count, card utilization | `balance_id` | `month_end` |
| `loans` | personal, auto or mortgage loan: principal, rate, term, credit band, DTI and LTV at origination, status | `loan_id` | `origination_date` |
| `loan_payments` | scheduled installment: scheduled and paid amounts, paid date, days past due | `payment_id` | `due_date` |
| `card_disputes` | disputed debit or credit card transaction | `dispute_id` | `dispute_date` |
| `credit_risk_report_delinquency` | month and product (workbook sheet `delinquency`) | | `month_end` |
| `credit_risk_report_provisions` | quarter and segment (workbook sheet `provisions`) | | `quarter_end` |

Foreign keys: `customers.home_branch_id`, `accounts.customer_id` and `.branch_id`, `monthly_balances.account_id`,
`loans.customer_id` and `.branch_id`, `loan_payments.loan_id`, `card_disputes.account_id` and `.customer_id`.
`monthly_balances` has a row for each month an account is open at month end, so a closed account's last row is the month
before it closed. Loan status and the close dates are as of 2026-09-30. `loan_payments` holds every installment due from
2025-04-01 and the installments still open at that date; `days_past_due` counts from the due date to the paid date, or
for an unpaid installment to 2026-09-30 (to the charge-off date for a charged-off loan).

How the numbers were made, so a model has something to find. Customers get a credit score and income from their segment.
Each month a customer may be in a stress episode that ramps up over a few months: card utilization rises, balances and
inflows fall, overdrafts increase, and then installments are missed. A current loan misses its next installment with a
probability that rises with a weaker credit score, the customer's stress, card utilization and time; a missed installment
can be cured later or roll to the next due date, and a loan is charged off after 4 missed installments (6 for a mortgage).
Customers leave in some month with accounts that close the same or the next month; most show falling balances and inflows
in the four months before, a few leave without a sign. Disputes are likelier for card holders, customers with earlier
disputes and customers under stress. Six months before the published window are simulated so the window opens in a
realistic state.

### Prediction templates (Kumo)

| Template | PQL |
|---|---|
| `loan_default_90d` | `PREDICT MAX(loan_payments.days_past_due, 0, 90, days) >= 30 FOR EACH loans.loan_id` |
| `account_closure_90d` | `PREDICT COUNT(monthly_balances.*, 0, 90, days) = 0 FOR EACH accounts.account_id WHERE accounts.account_type = 'checking'` |
| `card_dispute_30d` | `PREDICT COUNT(card_disputes.*, 0, 30, days) > 0 FOR EACH customers.customer_id` |

All use the anchor time 2026-09-30T00:00:00Z. `loans.status` and `.closed_date` and `accounts.closed_date` are snapshots at
that date: a backtest at an earlier anchor sees them already filled in.
