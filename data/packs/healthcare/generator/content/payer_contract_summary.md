# 1. Purpose

This summary gives Revenue Cycle and Denials Management staff the contract terms that decide whether a denied claim
can still be appealed. It condenses the payer agreements and, for Medicare and Medicaid, the program rules. If this
summary and a signed contract differ, the contract controls.

# 2. How the appeal window works

The first-level appeal window runs in calendar days from the date on the payer's denial notice (the adjudication
date in the claims system). A claim is appealable on a given date if that date is on or before the notice date plus
the window. An appeal that arrives after the window closes is rejected as untimely and the balance is written off.
The window is the same for every denial reason, including "Prior authorization missing", "Medical necessity" and
"Coding error".

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medicare | Original Medicare (fee-for-service) | [[appeal.Medicare]] days from the date of the denial notice | Medicare Administrative Contractor, redetermination request | 180 days from the redetermination decision | 12 months from the date of service |

| Self-pay | Patient responsibility | Not applicable | Patient Financial Services handles billing disputes | Not applicable | Not applicable |

# 4. Working a denial

1. Denials Management reads each denial notice within 3 business days and assigns a reason code.
2. Denials that can be corrected (a coding error, incomplete documentation, a duplicate) are corrected and resubmitted
   as corrected claims where the payer allows, which does not use up the appeal window.
3. For any denial that needs an appeal, the appeal is filed no later than 10 days before the window closes, with a
   letter of medical necessity where relevant.
4. The claim's appeal status is updated when the appeal is filed and again when the payer decides.

# 5. Contract notes for the next renewal

- Cascade Mutual has the shortest window and the shortest timely filing limit in the network. Managed Care Contracting
  will ask for a first-level window of at least 90 days at the renewal that starts 2027-01-01.
- Northstar Advantage requires that every appeal of a "Medical necessity" denial include a peer-to-peer request. The
  payer's clock does not start until the request is logged in its portal.
- Evergreen Health Plan accepts corrected claims for 365 days; these are not appeals.

# 6. Escalation

Denials Management reports every week the denied claims that have not been appealed and whose window closes in the
next 30 days. The Revenue Cycle director approves any write-off over $5,000 before the window closes.
